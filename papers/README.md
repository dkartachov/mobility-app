## Not here

These are paywalled or couldn't be downloaded, so only their abstracts were used:

- [2] Nambiar 2024, knee to wall in 400 young women. Open access (CC BY-NC), but its DOI link goes to the publisher's home page. It's used only as a cross-check, and McBride's discussion quotes its figures.
- [6] Soucie 2011, the paper itself (*Haemophilia*). The CDC dataset above is its data.
- [7] Roach & Miles 1991, hip active range by age group. See "Better data wanted" in the main README.
- [9] Youdas 2005, straight leg raise by age and sex. See "Better data wanted".
- [11] Powden 2015, knee-to-wall measurement error. The figures used are in its abstract.
